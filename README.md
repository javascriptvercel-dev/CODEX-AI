## About

CODEX AI is a web platform for setting up a WhatsApp bot session and finding
tools and community plugins to customize your experience.

## What you can do

- **Create a bot session** and connect your WhatsApp account using a QR code or
  pairing code.
- **Explore community plugins** for utilities, entertainment, moderation, and
  more.
- **Submit plugins** for review and publication in the community library.
- **Use built-in tools** including a QR code generator, password and UUID
  generators, a character counter, URL encoder/decoder, code minifier,
  JavaScript obfuscator, URL shortener, bot base generator, and WhatsApp number
  checker.
- **Manage your account** and view your links and tool activity from your
  dashboard.
- **Get help** through the support pages and site assistant.

## Get started

1. Visit [codex-ai.site](https://codex-ai.site) and create an account or sign in.
2. Open the session page and follow the steps to connect your WhatsApp account.
3. Browse the [plugin library](https://codex-ai.site/plugins) and
   [tools](https://codex-ai.site/tools).
